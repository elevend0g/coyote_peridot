# Coyote Peridot — Stage Loot Tables (Custodian Reference)

*One themed loot table per Stage, independent of which encounter is rolled there. Every party finds this loot somewhere in the stage regardless of what happens — weave the "how" into whatever encounter played out. A rival's dropped gear, a thing pried out of a malfunctioning Automatta, a stash tucked somewhere the Station never expected hands to reach.*

---

## How to Use

- **Roll 1d10** once per Stage, separate from the encounter roll. Consult that Stage's table below.
- Loot tier always matches the **Level's Power Tier** — Level 1 loot is P1 value, Level 4 loot is P4 value. This holds regardless of the party's actual Power Tier when they find it (standard Kit rules: a character can only access mod slots up to their own Power Tier — see *Equipping Higher-Tier Kit*).
- Two named items in a row share the same mechanical effect — pick whichever fits the fiction, or let the party's approach to the encounter decide which one turns up.
- Skill Items grant a **Reroll (Chaos Roll)** restricted to the named Skill only — a Content Module extension permitted under standard Reroll rules.
- The joke entry has **no mechanical effect**. Keep it that way even if the party gets creative.

## Mob Drop Tables

_A universal, tier-based table — separate from Stage Loot. Roll once when a named hostile (a rival operative, a corrupted Automatta, a security unit) goes down in Combat. Use judgment on scale: roll once per notable kill, not once per faceless mook in a crowd scene, and once for the culminating unit of a Wave rather than every spawn along the way. Loot tier always matches the encounter's Power Tier — Level 1 mobs drop P1, Level 5 mobs drop P5, regardless of the party's own Power Tier (standard Kit and mod-slot access rules apply)._

_Rows 1–9 vary in category so a single table covers weapons, armor, and mods of every type in one roll — including, now, a rare chance at a full Primary weapon off a mob that was carrying one. Row 10 is the joke entry, trimmed down to a 10% weight (half the Stage Loot Tables' rate — mob drops lean more mechanical, less incidental). Flavor alternates between "stripped from a fallen operative" and "salvaged from a broken unit" — pick whichever fits how the mob went down._
